### [VideoX](https://github.com/aveshk278-glitch/VideoX) — Android Video & Audio Downloader

[![Latest Release](https://img.shields.io/github/v/release/aveshk278-glitch/VideoX?label=VideoX&style=flat-square&color=7F52FF)](https://github.com/aveshk278-glitch/VideoX/releases/latest)

Download public videos and audio in the quality you pick — up to 4K — with an honest quality list and no silent downgrades.
